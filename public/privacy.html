Contractor Desk Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026. Privacy contact: info@getcontractordesk.com.

Information we collect

We process account names, email addresses, company profiles, team invitations, project and client contact information, estimates, financial entries, photos, notes and other content you enter. Application and hosting systems also process technical information such as request times, IP addresses, errors and session identifiers for operation and security. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes.

How we use information

We use this information to operate accounts and company workspaces, save and display records, support collaboration and shared portals, deliver account emails, investigate problems and protect the service. Account and service communications may be sent to your email. You can request that optional product announcements stop by contacting us.

Service providers and AI

Railway hosts the application and PostgreSQL database. Google Workspace processes account email sent from our configured mailbox. When you use an AI feature, the content submitted for that feature, including relevant job context, is sent to the configured provider, OpenAI or Anthropic, to generate a response. Avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information. These providers process information under their applicable terms and policies. When you set a password, we send only the first five characters of its SHA-1 hash to Have I Been Pwned’s Pwned Passwords service and compare the returned hash suffixes locally. Your password and its complete hash are not sent to that service.

Company members and shared portals

Authorized company members can access company records according to the service’s access controls. When you create a client or subcontractor share link, people holding that link can access the information and actions permitted by that link. You are responsible for obtaining permission to enter others’ information and for choosing who receives your shared links.

Cookies and local storage

The application uses a session cookie for sign-in and browser storage for interface preferences and its installable app shell. These support operation rather than advertising. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Retention and security

Records remain in the service while needed to provide your account and its features. Data may remain in backups or logs after removal from active records, subject to backup retention and operational or legal needs. We use authentication, company access restrictions and other safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can edit your records, manage team access, revoke shared links and export company data in the application. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion or account closure. We verify your identity and company authority before acting. We may retain information required for legal obligations, security or resolving disputes, and will explain relevant limits.

Children and updates

Contractor Desk is intended for adult business users, not children. Contact us if a child has provided personal information. We will post policy updates here and notify you of material changes through the application or account email.
